Doors with no favicon now get a brand mark instead of a plain monogram, for apps the dashboard can recognise — Ollama, Postgres, Redis, Grafana and around 40 other self-hosted and developer tools — matched from the process name, the command line, the supervisor's label or (as a last resort) a well-known port. Set `icon: si:<slug>` on any app to pick one yourself. The floating port-number badge in the middle of each door is gone: the port already read in the address line underneath, and the badge wasn't a click target, so it only cost clicks.

The dashboard's **Local domains** panel now offers setup's three tiers (No admin, System-wide, No prompts), the one recommended for your machine first, each with its address, what it will ask for and its `hallway setup --trust …` command to copy. No admin and No prompts have a **Turn on** button that runs setup from the dashboard after saying what will happen (on macOS, No admin asks once for your own Touch ID or password); System-wide still runs in a terminal. Once on, the header names the tier, e.g. `hallway.localhost · no admin`.
